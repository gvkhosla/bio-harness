# Evidence workbench surface

Mode: Operate / scientific review. Primary target: `web/index.html`; related: `web/app.js`, `web/styles.css`.

The user delegated selection and immediate delivery. This surface supports a short partnership demonstration, not live laboratory operation. Code-led implementation; no image-generation tools or separate reviewer agents are available.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An evidence ledger makes each recommendation traceable to a plate observation. Reject a chat-led dashboard and decorative KPI cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool near-white paper, dark slate ink, deep teal actions, plum concentration marks, muted amber warnings. Squared controls, tabular measurement numerals, fine dividers, no decorative imagery.

STORY: Inspect a published example, trace the shortlist to its wells, reveal flags and uncertainty, then demonstrate refusal when reference controls fail. Download a non-executable scientific review brief.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact header; research question; three working regions for context, a large 384-well plate, and a selected observation/decision. Source and failure-scenario controls precede the plate. The signature interaction highlights every replicate of a selected sample and exposes exact values and flags. Mobile stacks the regions and scrolls only the plate locally. State changes are immediate; a short background transition emphasizes selection, disabled under reduced motion.

FORM: Runbook ledger, candidate 6; seed 7c564863. Other considered grounded forms: evidence workbench, conference poster, assay report, microscope stage, scientific atlas, source-diff review. The user asked us to choose and ship rather than reopen selection.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Challenger verdicts and retained disciplines

- Pixel metropolis: declined on scientific audience identification and product clarity; retain consistent well geometry and inspectable detail.
- Man-machine signal grid: declined on both axes; retain explicit, persistent state labels, not color-only meaning.
- Drum-machine row: declined on both axes; retain direct keyboard-operable well selection.
- Cracktro depth queue: declined on both axes; retain a clear active selection without competing animation.
- Industrial label grammar: declined on both axes; retain literal action labels and artifact attribution.
- Deep-dive profile: declined on both axes; retain exact provenance through each evidence depth.

## Truth boundaries

Published example versus injected failure must remain visible in every state. The original file stays unchanged. No claiming provider-qualified QC, statistical significance, independent replication, real prices, live ordering, customer endorsements, or new biological discoveries. Browser has no private workspace access. Approval proof is a separately labeled synthetic Harness self-test.
